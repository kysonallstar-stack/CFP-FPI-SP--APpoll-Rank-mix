# Backtest: 2025 (tuning) and 2024 (holdout)

Generated by `python -m src.backtest`; raw numbers are in `reports/backtest.json`.

## Setup

- For every week *n*, ratings use only games and polls through week *n*, then
  predict week *n+1* FBS-vs-FBS games.
- **SP+ and FPI have no weekly history in the API**, so the metrics side is a
  stand-in: CFBD's weekly Elo plus our own margin rating (ridge SRS, margins
  capped at 28). "Metrics only" plays the role of "SP+ alone".
- Vegas = median closing spread across sportsbooks. It's a reference, not a model.

What this shows:

- **Blend vs. metrics only:** about the same overall. The poll helps a little
  between conferences (+1.3 to +1.7 points of pick accuracy), where computer
  ratings have the fewest shared opponents to compare teams on.
- **AP alone** is much worse. Only 25 teams are ranked, so most games are
  between two "unranked" teams.
- **Vegas** is about 1 point better in MAE but picks winners about as often.
- **Predicted margins are too large** (slope 0.73–0.81). Shrinking them to 80%
  lowers MAE by 0.25–0.45 points, more than any poll-weight setting. This is
  measured on the Elo+SRS stand-in and may not hold for SP+.

there is says the poll helps early (it carries preseason knowledge) and not
late. That may not carry over to SP+/FPI, which include their own preseason priors.

| 2025 | 4 | 0.168 | 0.153 |

Log loss improves at every checkpoint in both seasons. Log loss punishes
confident misses, so this confirms the old odds were overconfident. Brier
score is about the same.

## Committee approximation

Fitted to the committee's final 2024 and 2025 rankings:
`score = metrics rating - L * losses + C * (conference champion)`.

| Setting | Rank error (sum over the top 25) | Top-12 overlap (2025, 2024) |
|---|---|---|
| Current: L=3, C=2 | 245 | — |
| Best: L=8.5, C=0–0.5 | 193 | 9/12, 10/12 |

Anything from L=6.5 to 9 is nearly as good. The committee punishes losses
far more than computer ratings do, and gives little extra credit for a title
beyond the loss it avoids.
